Heart Zones

Support

Heart Zones turns the heart rate data your Apple Watch already records into a simple weekly cardio score.

How points work

Each minute in a heart rate zone earns points toward your weekly goal (150 by default):

  • Zone 1 (very light): 0 points
  • Zones 2–3 (light, moderate): 1 point per minute
  • Zones 4–5 (hard, maximum): 2 points per minute

Zones are based on your maximum heart rate (220 − your age, from Apple Health). You can set your own zone ranges in Settings.

Common questions

Why do I have no points? Heart Zones needs heart rate data in Apple Health, usually from an Apple Watch. Check that Heart Zones can read Heart Rate in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Heart Zones. Points are counted when heart rate is sampled frequently, as during a workout.

Why are my zones different from another app's? Apps use different formulas. If yours differ, set custom zones in Settings to match.

Does my data leave my phone? No. See the privacy policy.
