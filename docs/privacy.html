Privacy Policy

Heart Zones · Effective October 5, 2026

Heart Zones shows how much time you spend in each heart rate zone and tracks your progress toward a weekly cardio goal. It is built so that your health data never leaves your iPhone.

Data the app reads

With your permission, Heart Zones reads two things from Apple Health:

  • Heart rate samples, to calculate time spent in each zone.
  • Date of birth, to estimate your maximum heart rate (220 − age) for the default zones.

Heart Zones never writes data to Apple Health. You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps → Heart Zones, or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

How the data is used

Health data is processed entirely on your device to display your zones, points and progress. Your settings (weekly goal, week start day, custom zones) are stored only on your device.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, upload, store or transmit your health data or any other personal information to any server.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • We don't sell or share data with third parties.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Not medical advice

Heart Zones is a general fitness tool. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program.

Children

Heart Zones does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes
